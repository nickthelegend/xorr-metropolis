Triage:
- arbitrary-from in transferFrom (XorrDelegation._spend/_close): by design — the owner's registered delegate or agent pulls
  the owner's own token, inside the owner's daily cap, to a venue the owner allowlisted, with the minimum out measured on
  the owner's balance. Access is checked first (_checkAgent).
- reentrancy (_callVenue, _spend, _close): effects precede the external call (the day's tally is written before the pull
  and the venue call); a venue is not the delegate, and a re-entrant spend meets the updated cap. What remains is the
  event emitted after the call (reentrancy-events): informational.
- uninitialized-state XorrAuditAnchor._anchors: false positive — written through a storage pointer (`series.push`).
- divide-before-multiply KuruVenue.sell: intended — rounds the sale down to whole lots of the book's size precision.
- unused-return (approve, Kuru's market order results, getMarketParams fields): approve on USDC/WMON returns true or
  reverts; KuruVenue measures what it delivered by balance difference, not by Kuru's return value; the unused tuple fields
  are not needed.
- missing-zero-check (TestVenue, ReceiverTemplate.setExpectedAuthor): TestVenue is a test fixture contract; zero is the
  documented "disabled" value in Chainlink's ReceiverTemplate.
- timestamp, assembly, low-level call, naming, solc version, missing inheritance: informational or deliberate (day
  boundaries and expiry are timestamps; EIP-1153 transient storage and revert bubbling need assembly).
No high-severity finding remains unexplained; no code change was needed.
